{{ banner("research", "") }}

Each tier has its own research projects. Solar and Kwazaari research are studied at their own research benches, and each bench needs a Kylar-Xar effect computer linked to it, and from Kwazaari on, the bench of the tier before as well. Linked benches also speed up each other's research. The last Kwazaari project, dark pacts, needs the whole Kwazaari tree and unlocks the dark mirror. Tenebral research is never studied: it is bargained for at the mirror.

## Artifacts

{{ banner("artifacts", "") }}

Some projects can't be finished by study alone. Kwazaari and Tenebral research needs artifacts, which a colonist analyzes at the research bench of the tier before: Kwazaari artifacts at the solar fluid simulation array, Tenebral ones at the sigma-class sagittary autoscope.

## Strange signals

Once you have a powered solar fluid simulation array, it starts picking up strange signals. Send a colonist to investigate one and it may lead to a Kwazaari shuttle with a data drive for sale, the artifact that opens Kwazaari research. The signals stop once you've analyzed a data drive.

Not every signal ends well, and how a conversation ends can have consequences later.

## Dark pacts

Tenebral research isn't studied, it's bargained for. Every Tenebral project has a cost no bench could ever pay. The only way to finish one is a dark pact, made at the dark mirror. The last of them, the umbral research gate, is the ultimate pact: it unlocks the umbral spire.

A pact is a quest with a terrible price and seven days to pay it. Pay, and the research is done. Fail, and the mirror sends out a red pulse that punishes everyone it reaches.

{{ screenshot("dark-pact", "A dark pact offered at the dark mirror") }}

Umbral research is studied at the umbral spire. Its last project, the unlimited research gate, needs the whole umbral tree and opens the three unlimited projects, which an Umbral has to commune for at the spire.
