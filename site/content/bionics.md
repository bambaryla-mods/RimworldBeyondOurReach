The progression is simple:

- **<span class="t-solar">Solar</span>** bionics are better versions of the ones you know. They need Solar medicine and nanoclusters to install.
- **<span class="t-kwazaari">Kwazaari</span>** bionics are better again, and many of them combine several bionics into one part. They need Kwazaari medicine and picoclusters.
- **<span class="t-tenebral">Tenebral</span>** parts are self-forming organs and appendages that replace whole sets of body parts. They need femtoclusters and the best medicine, and they come at a price. See [the Tenebral road](tenebral-road.html).
- **<span class="t-umbral">Umbral</span>** parts need attoclusters, which can't be crafted.

Many implants also boost the mod's own work stats, like bionic crafting and neutronium forging speed. The items' boxes below are the ones you see with [Bionic Icons](integrations.html#bionic-icons) loaded.

## Tougher parts

With Elite Bionics Framework loaded, BOR body parts have more hit points than the natural parts they replace, so they take more damage before they break.

| Tier | Hit points |
|---|---|
| <span class="t-solar">Solar</span> | ×2.25 |
| <span class="t-kwazaari">Kwazaari</span> | ×3.25 |
| <span class="t-tenebral">Tenebral</span> | ×4.375 |
| <span class="t-umbral">Umbral</span> | ×6.065 |

Implants that sit on a natural part, like brain chips, eye lenses or mechanitor implants, leave its hit points alone. The exceptions are the kinetic dissipation mesh, the Kwazaari armor gland, the Tenebral omni armor gland and the Umbral brain, which toughen the part they're in by their tier's amount. EPOE-Forked rib implants add a flat +4 (Solar), +6 (Kwazaari) or +9 (Tenebral) hit points each to the ribcage, so a ribcage full of them stays breakable.
